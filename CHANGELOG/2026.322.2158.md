## Why the tag change cannot reach the wire

A struct tag is a decode key, so the honest question is *what decodes these types*. Measured — **every decode site is JSON, and none is YAML**:

```console
sdk/checkverb.go:51        json.Unmarshal(req.GetParamsJson(), &op)
sdk/deploy.go:31           json.Unmarshal(paramsJSON, &plans)
candy/plugin-cua/provider.go:52   json.Unmarshal(req.GetParamsJson(), &op)
candy/plugin-cua/kind.go:114      json.Unmarshal(paramsJSON, &dev)
```

A plugin's verb input arrives as `params_json` (protobuf bytes) and is decoded with `encoding/json`; `json.Unmarshal` reads the `json:` tag, which is unchanged on every field. The `yaml.Unmarshal` calls in `sdk` belong to `candywalk` front-matter and deploy-file documents and never touch a params type. So the new `yaml:` keys are **inert at the wire** — and that is why this change is `!`-free, stated as a measurement rather than as "wire keys unchanged".
